## About

Postgraduate student at the University of Warwick🇧(🇬🇧) with a background in scientific computing and applied machine learning. I am starting my career in empirical AI safety research, trying to understand how and why language models fail in ways that matter. My interests are wider than any single phenomenon: I'm drawn to the whole project of understanding emergent capabilities and what models actually encode internally, whether that's misaligned goals, deceptive tendencies, or representations of concepts like emotion. I want to connect that understanding to questions of detection, monitoring, and intervention. 

I am exploring research fellowships as a first step, and plan on applying to PhD programs for 2027 entry. Location isn’t a constraint, I’d be excited to embed myself anywhere that has exciting safety research.
