## Outstanding source ambiguities

No unresolved mathematical claim is needed for the narrative, but the original empirical reports remain unverified: Tersoff sensitivity rankings, sparse recovery numbers, GP test MAE, ethanol/propanol evaluations, trained ODE extrapolation, MNIST1D generation and banana-posterior ESS. Reproduction requires the exact notebooks, data, solver tolerances, seeds and training settings. They are labelled source reports where retained.

Supplemental calculations are teaching examples, not replacements for missing lecture evidence. Tests support their stated finite cases and invariants; they are not proofs of the general GP, PCA, MCMC or neural-network theories.
