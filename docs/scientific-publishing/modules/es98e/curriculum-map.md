# ES98E curriculum and source coverage

All 26 questions in the 934-line [original source](source-viva.md) were read before rewriting. That file preserves the pre-edit source verbatim. No other ES98E source, slide deck, notebook or dataset was available. Part B (questions 20–26, lectures 1–4) now precedes Part A (questions 1–19, lectures 5–8). The public index is `_notes/es98e.md`; each chapter below is a separate MDX-compiled `.md` file under `_notes/es98e/`.

Entry prerequisites: real vectors and matrices, multivariable differentiation, eigenvectors, positive matrices, probability densities, expectations and covariance. No neural-network, GP or variational-inference knowledge is assumed. Basic probability is stated as an entry prerequisite so Part B can introduce Bayesian regression without relying on Part A.

## Source-specific examples and evidence boundary

Tersoff screening and the 5-of-100 Lasso demonstration are retained as attributed reports in sensitivity; the 25-point vacancy-energy GP and 0.04 eV MAE in Gaussian processes; ethanol/propanol graph demonstrations in symmetry/graphs; the supplied analytic ODE in differential equations; MNIST1D/pPCA sampling in latent geometry; the banana ESS comparison in inference. Their absent code/data prevent reproduction. No newly generated figure represents those experiments.

Supplemental derivations: centred-Gaussian quadratic propagation; explicit rank-deficient feature example; exact one-observation GP; scalar residual-gradient caveat; force/translation proof; residual-to-error bound; exact pPCA posterior; exact normal VI optimum and KL gap. These additions follow the stated models and are distinguished from source empirical reports. See the correction ledger for assumptions and primary references.
